The TokenAI Concept

Integrating AI into high-stakes assessment requires a balance of security, ethics, and critical thinking. Our framework treats the LLM as a finite, monitored resource.

Digital Sovereignty

Institutions deploy centralized, secure AI platforms internally. This avoids dependency on external tech giants and ensures absolute data privacy for student sessions.

Token-Based Assistance

Usage is limited via a token system. Professors set a specific budget, forcing students to strategically decide when a hint or verification is truly necessary.

Environmental Impact

Real-time carbon tracking educates students about the physical cost of AI. Minimal usage is rewarded with grade bonuses, encouraging mindful interactions.

Security Architecture

  • No File Uploads: Students must formulate their own prompts, requiring active thinking.
  • Aggregated Models: Teachers select models per specific subject needs.
  • Locked Kiosk: Operates through a secure browser (SEB) to block external internet access.

Implementation

Our model ensures that students lacking fundamental understanding still struggle. By restricting tokens (like limiting a cheat sheet), we prevent students from delegating all reasoning to the algorithm.

Teachers gain flexibility: a large budget might test prompt engineering and critical analysis, while a small budget tests basic troubleshooting or knowledge retrieval.

Post-Exam Analytics

After the session, all prompts are accessible to teachers. This data allows for a deep analysis of student reasoning and their interactions with AI.
